Keep in Touch

The people who matter, on a rhythm you choose. Each week it suggests a few hellos and tells you why each one showed up. Private notes and important dates help you prepare. Everything stays in this browser on this device: nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is ever sent for you.

      Recovered data

      Saved data on this device could not be read, so the tool started fresh. The unreadable copy is kept here so nothing is lost.

      Where your data lives

      No account, no server, no contact syncing. Clearing this browser's site data deletes it, so export now and then.

      Sample people

      Nine invented people to explore with. They are marked as samples and never exported.

      Import

      A Keep in Touch export (.json), or a .csv with a header row. CSV columns: name (required), cadence, cadenceDays, lastContacted, birthday, notes, topics (separate with ;). Rows with problems are skipped and listed; the rest still import.

      Delete everything

      Removes every person, note and date from this browser. This cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
