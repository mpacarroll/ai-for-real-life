# Engine pipeline

Applies to the morning beat and the evening close.

Added 2026-10-26 after the staleness audit in `engine/README.md`.

- The brief is generated by `engine/build_brief.py` from `$ENGINE_DATA_DIR/inputs/<date>.json` and `$ENGINE_DATA_DIR/state.json`. Never edit yesterday's HTML into today's page.
- This repo is public. The ledger, inputs, reports and built pages hold personal data and live only in personal-hq (private), under `engine/`. Never commit them here; `engine/.gitignore` enforces it.
- Follow `engine/gather.md` to write the input file. One file per day, committed to personal-hq with the updated `state.json` at the end of every beat.
- `state.json` is the memory. Mick's words close an item there before anything is ranked. A source repeating a closed item does not reopen it; only his word or a first-party confirmation does.
- Five open loops maximum. Items with no clock and no first-party basis park after six days into the single "Only you know these" line. Do not restate them.
- Acts come from calendar commitments and what Mick said about his day. Reminders, the Optional calendar and the leisure titles in `engine/config.json` are not commitments.
- Keep in Touch picks rotate on a five-day cooldown. Placeholders and do-not-initiate notes never surface.
- Read the screenshot before publishing. Publish at the same file path the evening close used so the URL holds.
- The recap names what was read and what was not. A connector that failed is reported, never skipped silently.
- Do not read `personal-hq/CTAS.md` or `hq/revenue/QUEUE.md` for the brief; they lag Otto and he already folds them into his email.
